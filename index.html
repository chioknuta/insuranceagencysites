Questions

Anything else, email hello@insuranceagencysites.com.

Do I have to pay it all up front?

No. The Agency site is $1,500 to build and $99 a month, or nothing down and $199 a month for the first year, then $99. You own it from launch day either way.

I already have a website. What happens to it?

It stays up while we build the new one at a private address. On launch day we point your domain at the new site. Your email is not touched.

Who writes the words?

We do, from your current site, your listings and a twenty minute form. You correct anything before it goes live.

Is this AI?

Partly. AI does the first draft and the repetitive build work, which keeps the price down. A person reads every page, you approve every page, and nothing about your agency is invented.

Is there a contract?

No. Every tier is month to month, with no minimum term and no exit fee.

What is not included?

Logo design, blogs, ads, ongoing SEO campaigns, social media and photography. Spanish, extra pages and email setup are add-ons, from $150.
