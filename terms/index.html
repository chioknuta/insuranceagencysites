Paying

  1. How you pay. By card, on the Stripe checkout page we link to. On Starter and on pay once, the build fee is charged when you check out. On nothing down nothing is charged at checkout, and your card is kept for the monthly.
  2. When the monthly starts. Your first monthly charge is taken 14 days after checkout, or on launch day if your site goes live later than that. You are never charged a monthly before your site is live.
  3. Nothing down, after month 12. After your twelfth monthly payment of $199, the monthly drops to $99 and stays there.
  4. Tax. Prices do not include sales tax. If a tax applies to your purchase, we add it and show it on your receipt.

If we do not have your site ready for your review within 5 business days of receiving your completed intake form, for reasons that are ours, you can ask for everything you have paid us for the site back, and we refund it within 10 business days. The care plan

  1. What the monthly covers. Hosting, the SSL certificate, daily backups, uptime monitoring, security and software updates, your domain renewed at cost with no markup, and small changes by email.

  2. Small changes. Live within 2 business days of your email. Something broken or offline is fixed as fast as we can, the same day where we can. A small change is one we can make in about half an hour that adds no new page and no new design work.

    In, at no extra charge: editing text on any page, swapping or adding a photo, updating hours, phone numbers or addresses, adding or removing a team member or a carrier, updating a claims number or payment link, adding a PDF, changing where a form delivers, a seasonal notice or storm banner, and fixing a typo or a broken link.

    Out, always priced before any work: new pages, new sections or a redesign, a new logo or brand, blog or article writing, translation, connecting your agency management system, CRM, quoting engine or payments, custom forms with conditional logic, online payment processing, SEO or advertising campaigns, landing pages for ads, anything that needs a designer, and anything that would take us more than about half an hour.

  3. Cancelling. Cancel any time, from “Manage your care plan” at the bottom of our website or by email. The care plan ends at the end of the month you have already paid for. There is no notice period and no exit fee. On nothing down, cancelling before your twelfth payment ends the payments too: the site stays yours and you owe nothing more.

  4. When it ends. We send you the complete site files, your content, your form settings and your DNS records, point your domain wherever you tell us, and help you move for 30 days. Your domain is already in your name, so nothing needs to be released.

  5. If we end it. We can end the care plan with 30 days notice by email, and clause 15 still applies.

What we need from you

  1. The intake form. About twenty minutes, linked from the page you land on after checkout: your legal name, logo, address, hours, team, lines of business, where the forms should deliver, and your domain details.
  2. Your review. You read every page before launch and confirm it is correct. We do not launch a site you have not approved. You get one round of changes before launch, and changes after launch go through the care plan.
  3. If you go quiet. If we do not hear from you for 10 business days during the build, we pause and email you. We hold your work for 90 days, ready to pick up the moment you reply. After 90 days we archive the project and send you what we built. The money back promise in clause 9 does not cover a delay caused by waiting on you, and a build fee is not refunded once the work is done.
