About the agency

An independent agency in Rockwall

Lakeside Insurance Group, Inc. is an independent insurance agency at 510 Turtle Cove Blvd, Suite 103, in Rockwall, Texas. The State of Texas issued its agency license, number 7309, in October 1999, for property and casualty insurance and for life and health.

Russ Childers is the agency's president. His Texas insurance agent license was issued in 1992.

The office writes insurance for people and for businesses: auto, home, boats, motorcycles, RVs and umbrella policies on one side, and business liability, commercial property, commercial auto and workers' compensation on the other.
