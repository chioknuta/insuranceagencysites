Policy service

Call (972) 772-1913 for any of the requests below. A certificate request can also be sent in writing from the service center.

  • Request a certificate of insurance

    Send the holder's name and mailing address as they should read, what the certificate is for, and the date it is needed.

  • Add or remove a vehicle

    For a vehicle coming onto the policy, the VIN, year, make, model and any lender. For one coming off, the date it was sold or traded.

  • Add or remove a driver

    The driver's name, birth date and license number, and the date the change should start.

  • Get an auto ID card

    Tell the office which vehicle the card is for.

  • Change a mortgage company or lienholder

    Have the lender's name, the loan number and the mortgagee clause, written exactly as the lender gives it.

  • Report a claim

    Have the policy number, the date and place it happened, and any photos ready.

  • Make a payment

    Have the policy number ready and call the office to ask where a payment on that policy goes.
