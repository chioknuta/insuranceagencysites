Privacy

What happens to what you send us

This page covers this website only. It does not cover the information you give us when you buy or service a policy, which is handled under the notices your insurance carrier provides.

What the forms collect

The quote form and the certificate request form collect the name, phone number and email address you type, plus whatever you write in the message. That is sent by email to Apple Creek Insurance Agency through the service that runs this website's forms. This website does not keep a copy.

What we do with it

We use it to answer you. We do not sell it. It is shared with the carriers needed to quote or service your request, and with the vendors that run this website and send its email.

Cookies and measurement

This site sets no advertising cookies and runs no advertising trackers. If basic visitor measurement is switched on, it records pages viewed and the general region a visit came from. It does not identify you by name.

Asking what we hold

To ask what we hold, call the office on (330) 555-0179.

This site is not insurance advice

The pages here describe the kinds of coverage this agency can place. They are not advice about what to buy, and nothing on this site changes, binds or confirms coverage. Only the policy your carrier issues does that.
