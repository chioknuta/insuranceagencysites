Personal insurance

Coverage for your household

The personal side of the book is the same families as the farm and the store, and it runs on the same calendar. The house on the farm and the house in town, the pickup, the minivan and the first car, the boat that gets trailered up to Lake Erie once planting is done, the camper that comes out for fair week, and the umbrella that sits over all of it. Twenty-nine appointments means one call to Wooster puts a household in front of several companies instead of one. The number is (330) 555-0179, and a declarations page from the current policy, handed to Marcy, is enough to start a quote.

  • Home

    A homeowners policy is the house, what is in it and the owner's liability for what happens on the place. In Wooster that is the brick two-story near the college, the ranch out Cleveland Road, the newer house in Smithville with the pole barn behind it, and a good many houses that have been on the same policy since Walt wrote them. A carrier rates a house on where it is, how old it is, how big it is and what shape the roof is in, and carriers have gotten pickier about roofs every year, which we have seen.

    • Before a carrier quotes a house it wants the address, how old the roof is, the square footage and the year it was built, in about that order
    • A wood stove, a pool, a trampoline, a detached garage or a barn on the lot each gets its own question on the application, so mention them
    • When a loan is refinanced or sold, the mortgagee clause changes and the lender wants proof of insurance, and both come off Marcy's desk

  • Auto

    The auto policy is the pickup, the minivan and the sixteen-year-old's first car, and the people who drive them, on the same two-lane county roads as the buggies and the milk trucks. What a claim pays comes down to the limits printed on the declarations page, so that is the sheet we read first. The first car tends to show up in the spring of junior year, we have noticed.

    • We quote from the VIN on each vehicle and the name and birth date of everyone in the house who drives
    • Bring the declarations page off the current policy and we match it limit for limit before anybody changes anything
    • ID cards go in the mail when the policy is issued, or across the counter on Tannery Street if you are downtown anyway

  • Farm Home

    The house on a working farm is usually written on the farm policy alongside the barns, the machinery and the livestock, rather than on a standard homeowners form. Which form applies depends on the carrier and on whether the place farms for a living or keeps a few acres and a horse. Aaron handles both kinds.

    • A quote starts with the acreage, what is raised on it and whether the farm income is the household income
    • List every building on the property with its age, construction and what it is used for
    • A hobby farm with a few animals and a working dairy are quoted on different forms, so tell us which it is

  • Umbrella

    An umbrella is the policy that sits over the auto, the house and the farm and pays after one of those has paid out its limit. Carriers will not write one until the policies underneath carry the limits they want to see, so the umbrella gets quoted last and with everything else. Around here the reason people ask about one is usually a teenager with a license, a pond, or a produce stand out by the road, which we have seen more than once.

    • What the carrier asks for first is the liability limit on every policy the umbrella sits over, the auto, the house and the farm
    • A pond, a pool, a rental house in town, a young driver or a stand by the road each draws a question of its own
    • It gets priced with the rest of the household, not by itself, which makes it one call to (330) 555-0179 instead of two

  • Boat and Recreational Vehicle

    A boat policy is written on the hull and motor, the trailer it rides on and the liability once it is in the water. A camper or motorhome is written as a vehicle and as the place people sleep in it. Most of ours go north on US 250 to Lake Erie after first cutting and come back to a barn in Wayne County before first snow, and every September a few campers spend the week at the fairgrounds on Vanover Street instead.

    • A boat quote runs off the hull identification number first, then the length, the motor and the year and make
    • For a camper or motorhome, the VIN and whether anybody lives in it part of the year; the year, make and model come off the VIN
    • Say where it lives in season and where it winters, since the carrier rates on both

  • Renters

    A renters policy covers what a tenant owns and the tenant's own liability in a place somebody else owns; the landlord's policy has the building. We write them for the apartments along Beall Avenue by the college, the upstairs units over the storefronts downtown, the places out toward the OSU campus, and the rental houses in Orrville and Rittman, and a fair number of them for a student's parents back home, which is fine with us.

    • One phone call usually does it, start to finish
    • Have the address, the day the lease starts and a guess at what it would cost to replace everything in the apartment
    • Most carriers will put it on with the auto, and we quote it together and apart to see which comes out lower
