Personal insurance

Coverage for your household

Here is what the personal side of the office looks like in February. A house near the beach, a unit in one of the towers on Gulf Shore Boulevard, a boat on the lift behind it, two cars, a golf cart, and an umbrella policy written over the whole household. Come August, half of those people are back in Ohio, Michigan or Ontario, and we are on the phone with them anyway. When you call, we ask for your current declarations page. What you get back is a quote from more than one of the 26 carriers we are appointed with. The number is (239) 555-0147.

  • Home

    A single family house in Collier County: the structure, the contents, and the liability an owner carries. When you call about a house, the first things we ask are where it is, what year it went up, what the roof is made of and how old it is, and what the wind mitigation inspection found. Those answers do most of the rating on a coastal house, so we ask them before anything else.

    • When you call about a house, have the address handy, and know what year it was built, its square footage and the age of its roof
    • Carriers want a wind mitigation inspection, and a four-point inspection on an older house, so send both if you already have them
    • If your lender in Ohio wants evidence of insurance by email before a closing, or the loan gets sold and the mortgagee changes, that goes through this office

  • Condo

    Condo insurance covers the inside of the unit and what is in it, alongside the association's master policy on the building. Most of the condos we write are on Gulf Shore Boulevard, in Park Shore, along Naples Bay and on Marco Island, and about half belong to people who are here for the season.

    • Send the association's master policy declarations, or the name of the building and we will ask the management company
    • Tell us whether the unit is occupied year round, rented in season or empty in summer, because carriers rate each one differently
    • Units in older buildings sometimes need the building's inspection reports before a carrier will quote

  • Flood

    Flood is a separate policy from homeowners and condo, and we write it through the flood programs and carriers we are appointed with. It is quoted from the property's flood zone and elevation, and a lender often asks for it as a condition of the mortgage.

    • An elevation certificate speeds the quote up
    • We can pull the flood zone from the address if you do not have it
    • Flood policies typically carry a waiting period before they take effect, so the closing date matters when you call

  • Auto

    Florida auto insurance for a household's cars and everyone who drives them, whether the cars stay in Naples all year or come down on a transporter in November and go back up in May. We write it next to the house or the condo, so everything renews through one office and one phone number.

    • For each car we ask for the year, the make and model, and the VIN. For each driver, a name and a date of birth
    • Say which cars stay in Naples in summer and which go north, because where a car is garaged is part of the rate
    • What you get back once the carrier issues the policy is a set of ID cards, sent wherever you are that month

  • Boat and Watercraft

    Naples Bay is a few minutes from the office, and the boats we write live on lifts in the canals, in the dry racks on Haldeman Creek and at the marinas out toward Gordon Pass. When you call about a boat, we quote the hull and the motor, the trailer if there is one, and the liability once you are on the water. Nearly every carrier also wants to know where the boat goes when a named storm is coming.

    • We ask for the year and make, the length, the motor, and the hull identification number off the transom
    • Tell us where the boat sits in February, where it sits in August and where it goes during a hurricane watch
    • We write center consoles, pontoons, sailboats and personal watercraft

  • Umbrella

    An umbrella policy sits over the liability limits already on the cars, the house or condo and the boat, and it follows the same household. When you ask about one, we quote it in the same conversation as the policies underneath it, because the carrier wants to see those first.

    • Before an umbrella is quoted, the carrier reads the liability limits on the auto, the house or condo and the boat underneath it
    • A rental unit, a pool, a larger boat and drivers under 25 all draw extra questions
    • We quote it on the same call as everything else, so ask about it when you call (239) 555-0147 about the house or the boat

  • Golf Cart and Low-Speed Vehicle

    Naples runs on golf carts, from the garage to the clubhouse, around the community, and on the streets where a plated low-speed vehicle is allowed. We write the cart that never leaves the course and the low-speed vehicle that is titled and registered for the road, and the two are quoted differently.

    • Send the make, model and serial number or VIN, and say whether it is registered as a low-speed vehicle
    • Tell us where it is driven: the course, the community, or public roads
    • Some homeowners policies extend to a cart that stays on the property and some do not, so we read the policy before we quote a separate one
