Service center

Already a customer? Start here.

The policy is the start of it. What fills the day after that is the service: a marina on Naples Bay asking for a certificate before a boat goes in the water, a lender in Ohio wanting evidence of insurance by email, a car that has to be added before it leaves the lot, a storage letter for the boat when a watch goes up. All of it comes through (239) 555-0147, Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM. Below is what we ask for on each call, and what you get back.

  • Request a certificate of insurance

    Most certificates out of this office go to a marina, a restaurant's landlord, a condo building where a contractor is working, or the city for a charter permit. When you ask for one, we need four things from you. The holder's name and address, typed the way the holder wants to see them. What the certificate is for, meaning the lease, the job or the permit. Any special wording the holder has asked for. And the date the holder wants it by. What you get back is the certificate, sent to you and to the holder, during business hours.

    Request a certificate →

  • Add or remove a vehicle

    You bought a car, sold one, or a second car is coming down on the transporter for the season. Read us the VIN off the dash or the title, tell us the year, the make and the model, whether a bank holds a lien on it, and the day it goes on or comes off. The carrier gets the change the same day, and the new ID card follows.

    Call with the VIN →

  • Add or remove a driver

    A grandchild down for spring break who will drive the car, a new driver in the household, or someone who has moved out and comes off the policy. We ask for the driver's name the way the license prints it, the date of birth, the license number and the issuing state, and the date the change starts.

    Call with the license details →

  • Change a mortgagee

    The lender in Ohio sold the loan, or you refinanced the condo. Send us the new mortgagee clause and the loan number, word for word from the lender's letter. We send the endorsement to the carrier, and the lender gets its evidence of insurance from us by email, usually the same day.

    Call with the new mortgagee clause →

  • Get an auto ID card

    Lost the card, added a car, or the tag office is asking for proof. Tell us which car and where you are that month. The card goes out by email, or in the mail to Naples in February and to Michigan in July.

    Call for ID cards →

  • Report a claim

    Start with us. Give us what happened and when, and we find the carrier on the policy and get a claim number from them before you hang up. On a Sunday night with a tree through the lanai, the number on the back of your ID card picks up around the clock, and Monday morning it comes back to us.

    Call (239) 555-0147 →

  • Make a payment

    We do not take payments here. Your premium goes to the carrier, and each of our 26 carriers bills its own way. Call with the policy number and Yesenia or Kelsey tells you where that carrier takes payment, whether that is a website, a mailing address or a billing line.

    Call about billing →
