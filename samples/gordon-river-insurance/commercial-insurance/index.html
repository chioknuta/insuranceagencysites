Business insurance

Coverage for the business

The same office writes the business side, and Drew Castellano runs it: the restaurants and bars on Fifth Avenue South and Third Street South, the boatyards and marinas on Haldeman Creek and Naples Bay, the charter captains working out of Tin City and the city dock at Crayton Cove, and the condo and homeowner associations from Park Shore down to Marco Island. We are appointed with 26 carriers, so your business gets quoted with more than one of them. When you call (239) 555-0147 about a business, we ask what it does, where it sits and when the current policy runs out. What you get back is a short list of what the carriers want to see before they quote, and a call from Drew.

  • Restaurants and Bars

    We write restaurants and bars, from the sidewalk cafes on Fifth Avenue South to the waterfront places on Naples Bay and the year-round spots out on Tamiami Trail East. The package usually covers the building or the leased space, the kitchen equipment, the liquor liability and the people on the payroll, and we quote it with the carriers that write food service.

    • For a restaurant we ask for the square footage, the year the building went up, the roof, how many seats and what hours you keep
    • Send liquor sales as a share of total sales, the date of the last hood and fire suppression inspection, and payroll by job
    • Seasonal closings and shorter summer hours are worth mentioning, since some carriers rate around them

  • Marine Trades

    Boat repair yards, marinas, dry storage racks, detailers and the charter operators who run out of Tin City, Crayton Cove and Marco Island. We write the coverage on boats in a yard's care, the liability at the docks, the buildings and lifts, and the workers compensation for the crew, which is quoted differently when people work on or over the water.

    • For a yard or marina, send the number of slips or racks, the value of boats in your care at peak season, and the lift and equipment schedule
    • For a charter, send the vessel, the captain's license, the passenger count and the waters you run
    • Certificates for a marina lease, a tournament or a dockage agreement are issued from the office

  • Condo and Homeowner Associations

    The master policy on the building and the common areas, the directors and officers coverage for the board, the crime coverage on the association's accounts, and the flood and wind on the structure. We work with property managers and boards from Gulf Shore Boulevard down to Marco Island, and the renewal calendar runs around the season, when the boards are in town.

    • When a board or a manager calls, we ask for the appraisal, the year the building went up, the roof age, the wind mitigation report and the loss history
    • Send the building's most recent inspection reports and the reserve study if the board has them
    • We will walk the board through the renewal at a meeting in season if that is easier than a phone call

  • Commercial Property

    The building, what is inside it, the equipment and the stock: a strip of shops on Tamiami Trail East, a warehouse off Airport-Pulling Road, a boat storage barn. On the coast, four things set the rate on a building. Where it is, what it is built of, what the roof is, and how far it sits from the water. Wind is either inside the policy or written beside it, and we tell you which when we quote.

    • We ask for the address, the construction type, the square footage, the roof age and what year the building went up
    • Deed or lease? Say which, because the carrier writes an owned building and a rented space on different forms
    • If you keep a statement of values, send it with the request

  • Workers Compensation

    Workers compensation covers an employee hurt on the job. The carrier rates it on payroll, split by class code, and a line cook on Fifth Avenue South, a yard hand on Haldeman Creek and the maintenance staff at a condo building each carry a different one. Each class goes to the carriers that write it.

    • We ask for the payroll split by job, and how many people are on it in February against how many in August
    • If the account already has an experience modifier and loss runs, those come with the request
    • Call about a renewal early. These take the carriers longer than anything else we quote

  • Business Auto

    The trucks, vans and service vehicles titled to the business, and whoever drives them. A Naples business usually runs a short radius, Bonita Springs down to Marco Island, and the carrier asks about that radius before almost anything else.

    • We ask for the VIN on each vehicle, how far they run and who drives them
    • Driver license numbers and dates of birth are part of the quote
    • The ID cards and any certificate a landlord or a marina asks for go out from this office
