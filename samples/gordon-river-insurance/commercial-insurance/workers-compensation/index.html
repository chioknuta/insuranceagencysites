Business insurance

Workers Compensation

Workers compensation covers an employee hurt on the job. The carrier rates it on payroll, split by class code, and a line cook on Fifth Avenue South, a yard hand on Haldeman Creek and the maintenance staff at a condo building each carry a different one. Each class goes to the carriers that write it.

What a quote needs, and what to expect

  • We ask for the payroll split by job, and how many people are on it in February against how many in August
  • If the account already has an experience modifier and loss runs, those come with the request
  • Call about a renewal early. These take the carriers longer than anything else we quote

This page describes what this agency places. It is not advice about what to buy, and the policy your carrier issues is what sets your coverage. Anything you are unsure about, call the office on (239) 555-0147 and ask.

Ask about Workers Compensation

Whatever you type here lands in the Naples office, not in a call center. Tell us what you want a quote on, the date the current policy runs out, and how and when to reach you, and if you are up north for the summer, say where. Then a little about the thing itself. For a car, the model year, the make and model, and who will drive it. For a house or a condo, the address, the year of construction, the size in square feet and how old the roof is. For a boat, the year, make and length, and the dock or rack it sits in. For a business, what kind it is and the address it works from. If you have the current declarations page, attach it.
