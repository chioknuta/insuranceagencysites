About the agency

An independent agency in Naples

Gordon River Insurance Group is an independent insurance agency in Naples, Florida, in one office at 2860 Heron Reach Drive, Suite 4, off Tamiami Trail East. Gary and Renee Dunleavy opened it in 2004, the year after they came down from Dayton, Ohio, and it has stayed a five-person office by choice.

We do not belong to any one insurance company. The office holds appointments with 26 of them, and the two sides of the book, personal and commercial, are written at the same desk: the house, the condo, the boat, the cars and the golf cart, and the restaurants downtown, the boatyards on Haldeman Creek, the charter boats out of Tin City and the condo associations along the Gulf. In Collier County that matters more than in most places, because the companies writing coastal property change their minds from one year to the next, and when one pulls back we have somewhere else to take the policy at renewal without you changing agencies.

Gary still writes the personal lines himself. Renee runs the service side, the certificates, the mortgagee changes, the ID cards and the renewal calendar. Drew Castellano runs the commercial side, Kelsey Braddock takes most of the personal lines calls, and Yesenia Bracamonte handles billing and policy changes in English or Spanish.

About half our customers are seasonal, here from December to April, when Collier County adds close to a hundred thousand people, and back in Ohio, Michigan or Ontario the rest of the year. The other half are year-round families, the boats on the canals and on Naples Bay, and the businesses we write on the commercial side. Hurricane season runs from June through November and we plan around it the way everyone on the coast does. Renewals get lined up ahead of it, the boat storage questions get asked in May, and when a watch goes up for Collier County we stay reachable by phone at (239) 555-0147.

The people here

  • Gary Dunleavy

    Owner and agent

    Gary has been in insurance since 1996, the first seven years of it in Dayton, Ohio. He and Renee came down to Naples in 2003 and opened the office in 2004. He still writes the personal lines himself, and most of the seasonal customers have dealt with him directly for years.

  • Renee Dunleavy

    Owner, service and certificates

    Renee runs the service side of the office: the certificates, the mortgagee changes, the ID cards and the renewal calendar. If a marina needs a certificate before a boat goes in the water, or a lender needs evidence of insurance before a closing on Friday, it goes across her desk.

  • Drew Castellano

    Commercial producer

    Drew joined in 2015 and runs the commercial book: the restaurants and bars downtown and on Tamiami Trail East, the boatyards and charter operators on Haldeman Creek and Naples Bay, and the condo and homeowner associations from Park Shore to Marco Island. He grew up in East Naples and worked on a charter boat out of Tin City before he got licensed.

  • Kelsey Braddock

    Personal lines account manager

    Kelsey answers most of the personal lines calls. The grandson who just got his license and drives the car in season, the boat that moved to a lift on Marco Island, the car bought on Airport-Pulling Road that goes on the policy before dinner. She joined the office in 2018, and hers is the voice most people get when they dial the number.

  • Yesenia Bracamonte

    Service and billing, English and Spanish

    Yesenia joined in 2021 and takes the billing questions, the payment plans and the policy changes, in English or Spanish. She grew up in Golden Gate, and on a given day she is as likely to be on the phone with a restaurant on Fifth Avenue South or a marina on Naples Bay as with a household on the personal side.
