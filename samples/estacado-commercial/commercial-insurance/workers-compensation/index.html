Workers Compensation

We write workers compensation for the trucking companies, gins, farms and oilfield contractors that carry it, rated on payroll by class code. Drivers, gin crew, farm hands and roustabouts each fall under their own code, and getting the payroll split right is most of the work.

What a quote needs, and what to expect

  • Payroll by class code. Last year actual, next year projected.
  • The mod sheet and the loss runs, if the account has been rated before.
  • Comp goes to market slower than anything else in the file. Call early.

This page describes what this agency places. It is not advice about what to buy, and the policy your carrier issues is what sets your coverage. Anything you are unsure about, call the office on (806) 555-0128 and ask.
