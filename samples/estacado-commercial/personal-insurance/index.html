Personal insurance

Coverage for your household

Most of the personal side here belongs to the same families whose trucks and acres are on the commercial side. The farm and ranch house, the pickups and the gooseneck behind them, the family cars, the umbrella over all of it, and the motorcycle or travel trailer that comes out after harvest. Same office on the I-27 side of Lubbock, same people. One call moves a pickup from the farm policy to the personal auto and back. Call (806) 555-0128, or send the dec page you have now. Brenda runs it through our carriers.

  • Farm and Ranch Home

    A farm and ranch policy covers the house on the place, the barns, shops and pens around it, and the liability that comes with a working property. It is written differently from a town homeowners policy because the outbuildings, the equipment and the livestock are rated on their own schedule.

    • Address of the place, year the house went up, square feet, age of the roof. That is page one.
    • Barns, shops, pens: dimensions and contents on each one.
    • Say which fire district. Lubbock County rates one way, out past Tahoka or Post another.

  • Auto

    Personal auto: the household's vehicles and whoever drives them. Two pickups, a car for town, a kid who passed the DPS test last week. It sits in the same file as the farm and the trucking account.

    • VIN on every vehicle, plus year, make and model. Names and birthdates on every driver.
    • Snap a photo of the dec page you have now. Brenda rates off it.
    • ID cards print here. Same day, most days.

  • Pickup and Trailer

    The three-quarter-ton with the gooseneck, the flatbed that hauls the four-wheeler, the stock trailer that goes to the sale barn. Some of it rates on the personal auto policy and some of it on the farm policy, and we sort out which is which before we quote.

    • Truck VIN. Trailer VIN or serial, one per trailer.
    • What rides in the trailer, and whose. A neighbor's calves change the rating.
    • Pickup pulls for the business too? Say so. It decides which policy carries it.

  • Umbrella

    A personal umbrella runs above the auto, the farm policy and the house. Extra liability limit over all three. Carriers want a floor on those limits first. We quote it with the file, not on its own.

    • Liability limits off every policy underneath it. Auto, farm, home.
    • Every unit the household owns: trucks, trailers, four-wheelers, the place itself.
    • One call to (806) 555-0128. It prices with the rest of the file.

  • Motorcycle and Recreational Vehicle

    Motorcycles, side-by-sides, travel trailers and motorhomes. Around here they come out after the cotton is stripped and go back in the shop in the spring, and the carriers rate them on the machine, the mileage and where it sits between trips.

    • Year, make, model, VIN. Same as a truck.
    • Trailer or motorhome: weekend trips, or somebody's address half the year?
    • A side-by-side that checks pivots rates on the farm policy. One that goes to Buffalo Springs on Saturday does not. Say which.
