Restaurants and Food Trucks

Taquerias, food trucks, the panaderia on the corner and the catering trailer that works quinceañeras on Saturdays. Restaurant policies package the property, the liability and the equipment, and a food truck is written as a vehicle and a kitchen at the same time.

What a quote needs, and what to expect

  • A quote needs the menu in broad strokes, the hours, the seating, whether alcohol is served, and the square footage or the VIN of the truck
  • The commissary and the lot owner both ask for certificates. Send both names and both go out
  • Spoilage after a power outage and equipment breakdown are written as separate parts of the policy, so a quote lists each one on its own line

This page describes what this agency places. It is not advice about what to buy, and the policy your carrier issues is what sets your coverage. Anything you are unsure about, call the office on (210) 555-0163 and ask.
