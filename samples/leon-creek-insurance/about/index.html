About the agency

An independent agency in San Antonio

Leon Creek is an independent insurance agency on the Northwest Side of San Antonio, in one office on Granjero Drive off Culebra Road, just outside Loop 410. Rocío Pruneda opened it in 2011. She had spent nine years at a larger agency across town answering the phones, issuing the certificates and fixing the ID cards, and she learned there that most of what an agency does for a customer happens after the sale. She named it for the creek whose greenway runs past Culebra Road at Loop 410, a few blocks from the office.

The personal side is working families: the house, two or three cars, sometimes a manufactured home, sometimes a motorcycle, and the renters policy for the oldest kid's first apartment. What Rocío cares about most is the paperwork, the ID card printed before the tax office closes and the declarations page found and sent while the customer is still on the phone.

The commercial side started in 2014, when a customer's brother-in-law needed a certificate of insurance for a tile job by Monday and nobody else would call him back. Rocío's brother Ernesto joined that year, and he has built it one referral at a time: small contractors, landscapers and lawn crews, taquerias and food trucks, cleaning companies, and the trucks and trailers that go with all of them. Today we are appointed with 21 carriers, personal and commercial, and there are four of us in the office.

We answer at (210) 555-0163, Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday, 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM, because a lot of the people we work for cannot get away during the week. Customers come from the West Side and the neighborhoods along Loop 410, from Leon Valley up Bandera Road, from Helotes and Alamo Ranch out at Loop 1604, from the Medical Center, and from the families at Lackland on the southwest side. We write anywhere in Bexar County, but most of our customers are on the same three roads, Culebra, Bandera and Loop 410.

  • Paperwork done right the first time

    The certificate holder's name spelled the way the contract spells it. The mortgagee clause typed the way the lender wrote it. The ID card that gets a registration renewed at the tax office window on the first trip. Rocío did that for nine years at another agency before she opened this one, and it is the standard all four of us work to.

  • Both languages, all day

    Everyone in the office speaks English and Spanish. A quote, a claim call or a certificate request happens in whichever language the customer walked in with, and the paperwork goes out in the one the carrier and the lender read.

  • The certificate by Monday

    A contractor who cannot start until the general contractor has a certificate is losing money by the hour. Certificate requests go to the front of the line here, and when the policy allows it they go out the same day.

  • Several carriers from one call

    No carrier owns this office. Twenty-one of them have appointed us, personal and commercial, so the house on Culebra Road or the tile crew's two trucks gets rated with several companies from one visit, and the calling around happens at our desk, not yours.

The people here

  • Rocío Pruneda

    Owner and Agent

    Rocío opened Leon Creek in 2011 after nine years as a customer service representative at a larger agency across town. She still takes the desk most mornings and writes the personal lines, and when a title company calls about a Friday closing, or a lender wants its clause typed a particular way, the call goes to her. She grew up on the West Side and has never worked outside San Antonio.

  • Ernesto Pruneda

    Commercial Producer

    Rocío's brother joined the office in 2014 to build the commercial side, and most of it still comes in the way it started, one contractor telling another. He writes the general liability, the business auto and the workers compensation for the small trades, lawn crews, food trucks and cleaning companies, and he reads the insurance page of every contract before a certificate goes out.

  • Daniela Oyervides

    Service Agent

    Daniela is licensed and runs the service desk on the personal side. The customer standing in a dealer's finance office on Bandera Road who wants the new car on the policy before driving off the lot, the sixteen-year-old whose DPS letter just came, the renewal that came back higher and has to be shopped again, the claim that has to be reported to the carrier. She works in English and Spanish, and most people who call the office get her first.

  • Yolanda Carrizales

    Office Assistant

    Yolanda is in the office part time, mornings and Saturdays. She prints ID cards, takes the payment questions, walks customers through their carrier's billing line in either language, and keeps the certificate holder list current so the renewal certificates go out on time.
