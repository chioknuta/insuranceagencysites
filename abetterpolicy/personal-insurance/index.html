Personal insurance

Coverage for your household

Auto, homeowners, renters and flood insurance, quoted from the Lauderhill office with the carriers A Better Policy is appointed with. Call (954) 804-3898 or send a quote request.

  • Auto insurance

    A personal auto policy lists the vehicles and drivers it is written for. What it pays on a claim depends on the coverages, limits and deductibles on the declarations page.

    • Liability, collision and comprehensive
    • Requests to add a driver, or to add or remove a vehicle
    • Requests for auto ID cards and declarations pages

  • Homeowners insurance

    Homeowners insurance is written on a house the owner lives in, the things inside it and the owner's liability. Flood is not part of a standard homeowners policy and is usually written as a separate policy.

    • The house and other structures
    • Personal belongings
    • Personal liability

  • Renters insurance

    Renters insurance is for a tenant's belongings and personal liability. It does not cover the building itself, which is the owner's to insure.

    • Belongings
    • Personal liability
    • Extra living costs after a covered loss

  • Flood insurance

    Flood is usually its own policy, separate from homeowners or renters insurance. The price depends on the property and where it sits.

    • Building coverage
    • Contents coverage
