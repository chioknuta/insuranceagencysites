Policy service

Any of these can be started with a call to (954) 804-3898, Monday to Friday from 9am to 6pm. Certificate requests can also be sent online.

  • Request a certificate of insurance

    Give the holder's name and address, and any wording the holder requires.

  • Add or remove a vehicle

    Have the VIN ready, along with the date the change should take effect.

  • Add a driver

    Have the driver's full name, date of birth and driver license number.

  • Get an auto ID card

    Say which vehicle the card is for and where it should be sent.

  • Get your declarations page

    A lender or a landlord may ask for one. Say which policy it is for.

  • Report a claim

    Report a claim to the insurance company that wrote the policy. If you cannot find how to reach it, call the office on (954) 804-3898.

  • Make a payment

    If you are not sure where to make a payment, call the office on (954) 804-3898 with your policy number.
