Business insurance

Coverage for the business

Commercial auto, general liability and workers compensation for businesses, from the same Lauderhill office. Send the type of business and what you carry now.

  • Commercial auto

    Commercial auto is written on the vehicles a business uses and the employees who drive them on the job. Which vehicles and drivers it responds for depends on how the policy is written.

    • Liability and physical damage

  • General liability

    Responds to third-party claims of injury or property damage that come out of a business's premises or its work. Landlords and contracts often ask for proof of it on a certificate.

    • Premises and operations
    • Certificate of insurance requests

  • Workers compensation

    Covers medical treatment and a share of lost pay for employees hurt at work. State law decides which employers have to carry it.

    • Medical and wage benefits
    • Employers liability
