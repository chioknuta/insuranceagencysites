How many additional customers would cover the cost?

Use your own first-year contribution per new customer: the commission you keep after the costs of winning and servicing that customer. This is a break-even calculation, not a forecast of leads or sales.

Additional customers needed in year one

Enter your amount to calculate.

Build fee + 12 monthly care payments, divided by your contribution per customer, rounded up. Domain, tax and add-ons are extra. Count only customers gained because of the website.
